Connect to Signal

Link to your own account. rssignal becomes a secondary device, like Signal Desktop, and its messages come from you — which is also why your phone stays silent for them. Press the button, then scan the code from Signal on your phone, under Settings → Linked Devices → Link New Device.

Or give rssignal a number of its own, so its messages arrive from someone else and your phone notifies you. The number is the configured account, —, and it only has to receive one text. Never do this with the number your own Signal runs on: it would be logged out.

  1. Open Signal's captcha page and solve it.
  2. Copy the address of the Open Signal link it ends with — right-click or long-press it, Copy link. It starts with signalcaptcha://.
  3. Paste it here within a few minutes and ask for the code.

The PIN stops whoever gets this number after you from taking the account over. If the number already has one, type that one.

The name its messages come from. For an account of rssignal's own; emoji are fine. People see the new name once it next writes to them.
